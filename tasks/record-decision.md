# record-decision — record a decision

**When:** the user or a participant decides something: «решили», «записываем решение».

**Input:** the decision, its date, who decided, the basis.

**Steps**
1. Write the decision in the context with date, decision role, basis and whether it replaces an earlier decision.
2. Mark the replaced decision as replaced; keep its text.
3. If the decision changes a result, plan a new version of that result.

**Result:** a decision entry that can be traced to its source.

**Done when:** a reader can tell what is in force and why.

**Do not:** turn a proposal, a discussion or silence into a decision. Rules: [lifecycle.md](../references/lifecycle.md).
